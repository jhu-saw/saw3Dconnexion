1.0.0 (2026-08-22)
==================

* First release of the SAW 3Dconnexion component.
* Supports 3Dconnexion SpaceMouse and Spaceball devices through the cisst
  component interface.
* Includes ROS 1 and ROS 2 integration, configuration files, and examples.
